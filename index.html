❓ South African CV FAQs

How long does it take to receive my completed CV? ▼
Most CV orders are completed and delivered as print-ready PDF files via WhatsApp or Email within 1 to 3 hours after receiving your information.
How do I pay for my CV package? ▼
We support simple local payment options including Capitec Pay, EFT, eWallet, and cash deposit transfers.
I only have my Matric results paper—can you build a CV? ▼
Yes! Simply take a clear photo of your NSC Matric statement or report and send it to us on WhatsApp. We will extract your technical subjects, language proficiency, and qualifications accurately.

Yes. Both Classic and Modern formats use standard, clean typography and logical sections without broken tables or unnecessary symbols so HR recruitment systems can read them seamlessly.
